RAeS Adelaide – 8 October

Presentation & endorsement: Royal Aeronautical Society

On 8 October Toby and Brian presented "Decarbonising Global Freight with Cargo Airships" to the Royal Aeronautical Society, Adelaide Chapter. Toby ran the EAS fleet calculator live during the talk (calc.electricairshipping.com).

The RAeS provided a small formal endorsement of our project and supports the ongoing research into our Sunship concept:

"The Sunship concept represents the type of bold thinking that can stimulate valuable discussion, encourage new avenues of research, and inspire collaboration across industry, academia and government."

"The Adelaide Branch therefore welcomes and encourages the continued technical investigation and evaluation of the Sunship concept and looks forward to following its future development."

James Blagg CSC FRAeS, Chair, Adelaide Branch, Royal Aeronautical Society · 30 October 2026

It was a great night. It was a great response. We got lots of questions. We were told it was inspirational. We were introduced as 'like meeting the Wright brothers'. (!) Put it this way: In a room full of Adelaide's best and brightest aero-engineers, there was not a single question we couldn't answer to a level that felt satisfactory to the whole room. And afterwards, people were really happy to endorse us, chat over dinner, and the feedback was amazing. For EAS, and us personally, it was quite a big deal.

"Tonight was a rare moment in life where I learnt of new concepts in sustainable international airfreight, based on a whole new approach to design of super sized airships. The concepts are akin to first exposure to a jet engine, or a multi stage rocket."

James Blagg CSC, Chair of the RAeS Adelaide Chapter

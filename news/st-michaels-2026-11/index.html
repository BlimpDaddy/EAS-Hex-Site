St Michael's College – 2 November

Presentation: St Michael's College

On 2 November Toby presented the Sunship to students at St Michael's College, his old high school, and ran the EAS fleet calculator (calc.electricairshipping.com) on the big screen.

"Your presentation was fantastic! You pitched it at the perfect level for the students to engage and the length was spot on."

"So often we reach the end of a presentation and there are no questions, so the number of questions students had today really speaks to how well you presented and how interesting your work is!"

Jack Alberton, Leader of Learning: Science, St Michael's College

Toby also talked about the EAS project's links to decarbonisation, sustainability and a prosperous future, and about curiosity through life, and not knowing what you 'want to do' straight out of school. All in all, the presentation went really well, and it was a nostalgic and inspiring time.

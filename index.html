The problem

International shipping is locked in.

  1. 1

    Too slow, or too expensive

    Maritime shipping can take weeks or months. Air freight costs many times more, with chargeable-weight penalties.

  2. 2

    Too polluting

    Shipping emits 3% of total global CO₂ emissions, with no win/win decarbonisation pathway.

  3. 3

    Too little choice

    Nothing in between. Hub-and-spoke routing, and inefficiencies dictated by geography and physics.

The Sunship

A new choice, over the ocean.

  1. 1

    Affordable

    Days, not weeks, at a fraction of the cost of air freight.

  2. 2

    Sustainable

    Lifted by electrically heated air. No helium, no hydrogen, no emissions in flight.

  3. 3

    Adaptable

    Point-to-point routing, route optimization, dynamic spot markets. Freight goes where it needs to go.
